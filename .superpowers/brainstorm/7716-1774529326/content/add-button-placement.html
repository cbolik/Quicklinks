Where should the "Add Link" button live?

Consider both mobile (primary) and desktop usage

A

Fixed bottom-right FAB

A small floating "+" button always visible in the bottom-right corner, above the dot indicators. Familiar mobile pattern (like Material Design FAB). Stays out of the way but always reachable.

B

In the indicators bar

A small "+" button sits next to the dot indicators at the bottom. Integrated into the existing UI, doesn't add another floating element. Might feel cramped on small screens.

C

Top-right corner

A subtle "+" in the top-right of the page. Clean and unobtrusive, but less discoverable on mobile and harder to reach with one hand on large phones.
